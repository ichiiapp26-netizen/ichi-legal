Ichi Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

In short

In this policy, “we” means the developer of Ichi. Ichi works without an account, and almost all learning data stays on your phone.

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • Your learning stays on your device. Your preferences, progress, streak, and saved expressions are stored only on your phone.
  • Purchases go through Apple or Google. We never see your payment details. Our purchase provider, RevenueCat, receives your purchase history under an anonymous ID.
  • Privacy-preserving usage statistics and crash reports, through TelemetryDeck and Sentry. Never your name, contacts, location, or anything you type.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps, and we never sell your information.

Ichi keeps this information in your phone’s app storage. It is not sent to us.

Ichi Core can always be restored, through your Apple or Google account.

Purchases

Ichi Core is a one-time purchase made through the App Store or Google Play. It gives permanent access to the Ichi Core Collection and its enhanced learning features. Apple or Google handles the payment under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card or bank details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases and to let you restore Ichi Core. When you buy, restore, or open Ichi, RevenueCat receives:

  • your purchase history for Ichi (what was bought, when, and in which store)
  • a random ID that RevenueCat creates for this installation, not linked to your name or email
  • technical details that come with any internet request, such as your IP address, device type, operating system, and app version

We use this information to verify and restore purchases, provide Ichi Core access, prevent fraud, and understand purchase activity. RevenueCat processes it for us under its privacy policy.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To see which parts of Ichi help people learn and to fix problems, Ichi sends two kinds of data, without your name or contact details. Production analytics and crash reports are kept separate from preview and test data. Preview builds may send test signals to TelemetryDeck that are marked separately and are not included in production analytics, and their crash reports go to a separate preview environment in Sentry.

Usage statistics (TelemetryDeck). Ichi records a fixed list of actions:

  • opening the app, and starting and finishing setup (with your level and how many goals and interests you chose, not which ones)
  • viewing today’s expression, opening, saving, or unsaving an expression, and playing its audio (with the expression’s ID)
  • using Discover and its category filters
  • opening the Ichi Core page, and the outcome of a purchase or restore (for example completed, cancelled, or failed), never payment details

These are linked to a random ID that Ichi creates when you first open it. It is not your device ID, advertising ID, or purchase ID, and TelemetryDeck receives it only in hashed form. TelemetryDeck also receives a random session ID that changes each time you open Ichi. We use these to count things like how many people return after a week, never to identify you.

Crash reports (Sentry). When Ichi crashes or hits an error, it sends a report with the error, the app version and build number, and your device type and operating system version. Reports carry no ID for you, and we set Sentry not to store your IP address.

Neither service receives anything you type, your name, your contacts, or your location. You can turn off usage statistics at any time in Settings (Share usage statistics); Ichi then stops sending them.

What Ichi does not collect

Ichi does not collect, and does not ask for permission to access:

  • your name, email address, or phone number
  • your contacts, photos, or files
  • your location
  • your microphone or camera
  • your advertising identifier

Ichi shows no ads, does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.

Children’s privacy

Ichi is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child in a way that requires parental consent, we will take appropriate steps to delete it or otherwise comply with applicable law.

Your rights, changes, and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to how it is used. Because Ichi has no accounts, we usually cannot link information to you without an ID from your device. Contact us and we will help.

We may update this policy when Ichi changes. The date at the top shows the latest version, and we will point out significant changes in the app.
